\section*{Data availability}
The code, the benchmark with its annotated evidence bundles, the per-question verdicts and the ledgers of runs are available in the GitHub repository \url{https://github.com/ArashNasrEsfahani/AnyRAG}. Full per-question records and all post hoc runs are at \url{https://huggingface.co/datasets/ArashNasr/anyrag-results}; the benchmark is at \url{https://huggingface.co/datasets/ArashNasr/anyrag-benchmark}. % src: R12: HF README "Folders" table (test/, human_review/, protocol/, dev/, posthoc/S1..S17); paired statistics are recomputed from test/runs, test/replay, test/judge and human_review
In the results dataset, \texttt{test/} holds the frozen test, its paired statistics (\texttt{TEST\_RESULTS.md}) and the answers (\texttt{runs/}, \texttt{replay/}) and verdicts (\texttt{judge/}) of the six readers. The folder \texttt{protocol/} holds the rules fixed before the test and every change to them, \texttt{human\_review/} the human reviews, \texttt{dev/} the development record and \texttt{posthoc/S1} to \texttt{posthoc/S17} one post hoc experiment each. The prompts are in the code repository under \texttt{code/ragbench}. The cached responses of the language-model APIs are retained and are available on request.
